01 · The story so far

I started writing code in 2018 at BullxBear, building a USD-to-IRR currency exchange platform. The project was cancelled because of legal restrictions, but it taught me programming the hard way and showed me that a product is much more than its code.

From 2020 to 2022 I worked as a solo full-stack developer and delivered projects for clients in different industries. Freelancing taught me to own a project from the first sketch to deployment and to solve problems without waiting for anyone.

Since late 2022 I have been a senior front-end developer at Zaamdesign, leading projects with Nuxt.js and Next.js for clients in Canada, Germany and the USA. The work ranges from school management and festival websites to desktop tools built with Electron and mobile apps with React Native.

Alongside web work I have been building trading systems for years. I write MQL5 Expert Advisors and custom indicators, structured in clean layers so a strategy can be tested and swapped without rewriting the whole robot.

Lately AI tools are part of my daily workflow, from designing prompts to reviewing the code they produce. I am looking for a full-stack role where I can build products that scale.
